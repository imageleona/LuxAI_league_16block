Why the two lines differ so much. PFSP samples opponents by (1−winrate)^0.5, so it concentrates on whoever is beating you — and your own recent snapshots always are, roughly half the time. The snapshot line hovering at zero is the mechanism working as designed, not a failure. The anchor line is the one that carries information about absolute strength, because those four opponents never change.
